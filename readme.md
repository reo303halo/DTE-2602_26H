# Introduction to Machine Learning and AI — DTE-2602

Welcome to **Introduction to Machine Learning and AI (DTE-2602)**!

The course primarily follows a **flipped classroom** model. This means that students are expected to work with course material and assignments independently, while lectures and online meetings are used for discussion, explanations, problem-solving, and questions.

For more information about the flipped classroom approach, see the [Harvard Bok Center's introduction to flipped classrooms](https://bokcenter.harvard.edu/flipped-classrooms).

## Teaching and Online Meetings

Lectures and online meetings are held via **Microsoft Teams from Narvik**, at the same time as the classroom sessions.

**Classrooms:**

* **Tuesdays & Thursdays:** D3330
* **Fridays:** E1600

The online meetings will be recorded.

The meetings will be used for:

* Introducing new topics
* Reviewing exercises and assignments
* Going through solutions
* Discussions
* Questions and answers

> **Important:** Keep an eye on course announcements to stay up to date with meeting information, changes, and deadlines.

Although the **language of instruction is Norwegian**, some of the literature, exercises, assignments, and other course material will be available in **English**.
